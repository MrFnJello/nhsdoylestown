Dialysis Units

Local dialysis facilities.

The practice states that it currently sees dialysis patients at three units.

Liberty Dialysis – Doylestown

Located on Kelly Drive off Route 611. The facility opened in 2008 and the original site highlights amenities including wireless internet access, flat-screen televisions and heated chairs.

Unit phone: (215) 488-7226

Belmont Court Dialysis Center

Located in the lower level of Briarleaf Nursing and Convalescent Center in Doylestown. Dialysis patients placed at Briarleaf can receive dialysis at Belmont Court.

Abramson Center Dialysis

Located on Horsham Road near Lower State Road within the Abramson Center for Jewish Life. Rehabilitation residents can receive dialysis without traveling to another facility.

Kidney disease and hypertension care serving Bucks and Montgomery counties.
