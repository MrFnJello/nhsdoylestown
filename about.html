About Us

Medical care first.

A modern presentation of the practice philosophy from the original site.

Patient care without unnecessary friction.

In a healthcare environment shaped by changing medical rules and insurance requirements, the practice describes “medical care first” as its uncompromising philosophy.

One full hour is scheduled for a new patient’s first visit to allow time for a complete history and physical examination. Follow-up visits are by appointment, with a commitment to keeping the office schedule on time.

What that means for patients

  • One-hour initial visits
  • Follow-up care by appointment
  • Direct contact with office staff
  • 24/7 physician-on-call access for urgent issues

Kidney disease and hypertension care serving Bucks and Montgomery counties.
