Driving Directions

Directions preserved from the original site.

From New Jersey and east

From I-95 in New Jersey toward Pennsylvania, take Exit 49 for PA-332 toward Newtown. Continue through Newtown and follow PA-413 north, then US-202 toward Doylestown. Route 202 joins Route 611 South. Exit at Route 202 South / State Street and bear right. The hospital is two blocks on the left.

From the south

From Philadelphia, take I-76 West to I-476 North and continue to the Pennsylvania Turnpike, then follow the Turnpike directions.

From the north

From Allentown, take Route 309 South to Route 313 East. Follow Route 313 to Route 611 South, exit at Route 202 South / State Street and bear right. The hospital is two blocks on the left.

From the west

From Norristown, take Route 202 North to Route 309, continue on Upper State Road, then turn right onto 202 North. Continue approximately 1.5 miles to the Doylestown Hospital entrance on the left.

From the Pennsylvania Turnpike

Take Exit 343, Willow Grove. Follow Route 611 North toward Doylestown, exit at Route 202 South / State Street, then turn left onto State Street. The hospital entrance is the next driveway on the left.
